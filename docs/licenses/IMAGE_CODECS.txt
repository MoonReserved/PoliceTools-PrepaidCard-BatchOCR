Distributed runtime files are unchanged upstream files.

Corresponding source and build scripts: ../sources/image-codecs-source.zip
Includes libheif, libde265, libheif-js and libheif-emscripten source, plus the complete pinned UTIF source.
The upstream workflow uses Emscripten 3.1.61, USE_WASM=1, USE_UNSAFE_EVAL=0,
USE_TYPESCRIPT=1 and libheif's build-emscripten.sh (libde265 enabled, AOM disabled).
Follow libheif-emscripten/.github/workflows/emscripten.yml and dist-prep.sh; place
libheif source in its libheif subdirectory, then build. Pass the resulting tarball
to libheif-js/scripts/install.js to reproduce the browser bundle with esbuild.
To use a modified decoder, replace image-codecs/libheif-bundle.js, bump
OFFLINE_APP_VERSION in offline-assets.js and reload the local site/cache.
Reverse engineering for debugging modifications to these LGPL libraries is permitted.
